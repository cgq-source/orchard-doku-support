Orchard Doku 3D brings fruit-filled logic puzzles onto a colorful cube. Explore 1,000 3D puzzles alongside the 1,100-level classic campaign.

THREE CONNECTED FACES
Solve the top, front and right faces of 6x6, 7x7, 8x8 and 9x9 cubes. Each face needs one fruit in every row, column and colored region. Fruits cannot touch, including across shared edges. Rotate the cube to reason across faces; tap once to mark an exclusion and twice to place fruit. The first six puzzles in each size are free. Pro unlocks the rest. A first 3D clear earns 4 gold seeds, or 5 with Pro. Hints cost 6 seeds and fruit-placement help costs 12. Four separate Game Center leaderboards count first clears by size; replays do not add another clear.

A GENTLE START, ROOM TO GROW
Explore a 1,100-level campaign with 5x5 to 9x9 boards. Adaptive difficulty responds to your recent progress. Take on a separate daily challenge while keeping your campaign board and notes.

TIME TRIAL
Race through 7x7 to 10x10 boards with a six-minute starting clock. Every clear adds 20 seconds; a mistake costs 10 seconds. Two free runs each day, or unlimited runs with Pro. Compare your best score on Game Center.

CLASSIC TOOLS THAT EXPLAIN
Use marks, flags, undo, automatic exclusions and step-by-step hints. Replay the guided tutorial whenever you need a refresher.

GROW YOUR ORCHARD COLLECTION
Unlock ten orchard cards as you reach campaign milestones, with gold-seed rewards for each achievement. Browse your seed income and spending in one clear history.

10 GAME CENTER ACHIEVEMENTS
Reach classic milestones at 399, 699 and 999 first clears; collect 100, 300 or 900 fruits in a single completed Time Trial; and reach halfway in each of the four 3D sizes. Open achievements below the leaderboard trophy on the home screen. Game Center points are separate from gold seeds.

CONTINUE ACROSS DEVICES
iCloud sync automatically combines completed levels and best scores across devices using the same Apple Account. New installations enable sync by default; an existing choice to turn it off is respected. Play offline, then sync from the home screen when connected. Active puzzles are protected, and ordinary progress differences no longer require choosing one complete save. Both devices should use version 1.1.2 or later. Check sync status before changing devices or deleting the app.

PLAY FREE, OR CHOOSE EXTRA HELP
The first 66 campaign levels are free. Later levels can be permanently unlocked for 8 gold seeds each. Earn seeds by playing, or buy optional 120-, 420- or 1,200-seed packs. Packs can be purchased repeatedly and seeds do not expire. Use seeds for hints, revealing a fruit, extra hearts and level unlocks. Purchases require internet and an iCloud wallet.

The optional, one-time Orchard Doku Pro purchase unlocks all 1,100 campaign levels and all 1,000 3D puzzles, includes a first-purchase gift of 120 seeds, and adds first-clear reward bonuses. Restore eligible Pro purchases from Settings.

MADE FOR IPHONE AND IPAD

From version 1.1.2: iCloud sync is on by default for new installations or when no saved preference exists; a saved off preference is preserved. It can be changed in Settings > iCloud Sync. When enabled and iCloud is available, game snapshots are stored in your private CloudKit database under the same Apple Account. Completed levels are combined and best scores retain the higher result; active puzzles are protected and alternate records may be backed up locally. Reward records are reconciled conservatively; independent wallet balances are not added together. Older economic records that cannot be safely reconciled may still require a recovery choice. Going to the background only attempts to publish local changes; completion is not guaranteed. Use compatible versions on both devices and check sync status before changing devices or deleting the app. Game Center receives achievement identifiers and completion percentages associated with the signed-in player, in addition to leaderboard scores. This does not create a developer account or advertising profile.

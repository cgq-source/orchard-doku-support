Orchard Doku 3D · English

Orchard Doku 3D brings fruit-filled logic puzzles onto a colorful cube. Explore 1,000 3D puzzles alongside the 1,100-level classic campaign.

THREE CONNECTED FACES Solve the top, front and right faces of 6x6, 7x7, 8x8 and 9x9 cubes. Each face needs one fruit in every row, column and colored region. Fruits cannot touch, including across shared edges. Rotate the cube to reason across faces; tap once to mark an exclusion and twice to place fruit. The first six puzzles in each size are free. Pro unlocks the rest. A first 3D clear earns 4 gold seeds, or 5 with Pro. Hints cost 6 seeds and fruit-placement help costs 12. Four separate Game Center leaderboards count first clears by size; replays do not add another clear.

Place one fruit in every row, column and colored region. Fruits cannot touch, even diagonally. Mark the spaces you can rule out and find each solution with logic.

A GENTLE START, ROOM TO GROW Explore a 1,100-level campaign with 5x5 to 9x9 boards. Adaptive difficulty responds to your recent progress. Take on a separate daily challenge while keeping your campaign board and notes.

TIME TRIAL Race through 7x7 to 10x10 boards with a six-minute starting clock. Every clear adds 20 seconds; a mistake costs 10 seconds. Two free runs each day, or unlimited runs with Pro. Compare your best score on Game Center.

CLASSIC TOOLS THAT EXPLAIN Use marks, flags, undo, automatic exclusions and step-by-step hints. Replay the guided tutorial whenever you need a refresher.

GROW YOUR ORCHARD COLLECTION Unlock ten orchard cards as you reach campaign milestones, with gold-seed rewards for each achievement. Browse your seed income and spending in one clear history.

PLAY NOW, SYNC LATER Optional iCloud sync keeps classic and 3D progress, in-progress boards, the selected 3D size and face, play history, achievements and reward records across devices using the same Apple account. Gold-seed spending and rewards save locally immediately, even offline; synchronization runs outside gameplay. If both devices have new progress, choose a complete save after reviewing the two summaries; both copies are backed up before replacement. Sync before changing devices or deleting the app.

PLAY FREE, OR CHOOSE EXTRA HELP The first 66 campaign levels are free. Later levels can be permanently unlocked for 8 gold seeds each. Earn seeds by playing, or buy optional 120-, 420- or 1,200-seed packs. Packs can be purchased repeatedly and seeds do not expire. Use seeds for hints, revealing a fruit, extra hearts and level unlocks. Purchases require internet and an iCloud wallet.

The optional, one-time Orchard Doku Pro purchase unlocks all 1,100 campaign levels and all 1,000 3D puzzles, includes a first-purchase gift of 120 seeds, and adds first-clear reward bonuses. Restore eligible Pro purchases from Settings.

MADE FOR IPHONE AND IPAD Enjoy friendly fruit characters, optional music, sound and haptics, and iPad landscape layouts. Optional Game Center leaderboards compare total first-time campaign clears.

Available in English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Korean. Enjoy an optional dark mode and 3D fruit that stays upright as you tilt your device.

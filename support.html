OrchardDoku 1.2.1

This update describes version 1.2.1 and later; availability is shown in the App Store. Earlier versions may still use a separate iCloud wallet. Update both devices to compatible versions and confirm synchronization before changing devices or deleting the app.

Orchard Doku brings fruit-filled logic puzzles onto a colorful cube. Explore 1,000 3D puzzles alongside the 6,000-level classic campaign.

DOUBLE FRUIT, DOUBLE THE FUN
Explore 1,000 puzzles with two fruits in every colored region, row and column. Fruits cannot touch, even diagonally. Boards grow from 8×8 to 9×9 and 10×10, with a hands-on tutorial to get you started. The first 15 levels are free; unlock each later level permanently for 8 gold seeds, or play them all with Pro. A dedicated Game Center leaderboard counts distinct completed levels. Progress is included in iCloud sync.

THREE CONNECTED FACES
Follow one 1,000-level 3D journey with 6x6 to 9x9 cubes. Place one fruit in each row, column and colored region on every face; fruits cannot touch across shared edges. Rotate to connect clues, tap once to mark and twice to place fruit. The first 29 levels are free; later levels unlock permanently for 8 gold seeds each, or with Pro. A unified Game Center leaderboard counts distinct completed 3D levels. First clears earn 4 gold seeds, or 5 with Pro. Hints cost 6 seeds and fruit-placement help costs 12.

Place one fruit in every row, column and colored region. Fruits cannot touch, even diagonally. Mark the spaces you can rule out and find each solution with logic.

A GENTLE START, ROOM TO GROW
Explore a 6,000-level campaign with 5x5 to 10x10 boards. Adaptive difficulty responds to your recent progress. Take on a separate daily challenge while keeping your campaign board and notes.

TIME TRIAL
Race through 7x7 to 10x10 boards with a six-minute starting clock. Every clear adds 20 seconds; a mistake costs 10 seconds. Two free runs each day, or unlimited runs with Pro. Compare your best score on Game Center.

CLASSIC TOOLS THAT EXPLAIN
Use marks, flags, undo, automatic exclusions and step-by-step hints. Replay the guided tutorial whenever you need a refresher.

GROW YOUR ORCHARD COLLECTION
Explore 30 orchard groups through classic levels and claim existing milestone rewards. Tap your gold seeds to open the shop directly.

CONTINUE ACROSS DEVICES
iCloud sync automatically combines completed levels and best scores across devices using the same Apple Account. New installations enable sync by default; an existing choice to turn it off is respected. Play offline, then sync from the home screen when connected. Active puzzles are protected, and ordinary progress differences no longer require choosing one complete save. Both devices should use version 1.2.1 or later. Check sync status before changing devices or deleting the app.

PLAY FREE, OR CHOOSE EXTRA HELP
The first 100 campaign levels are free. Later levels can be permanently unlocked for 8 gold seeds each. Earn seeds by playing, or buy optional 120-, 420- or 1,200-seed packs. Packs can be purchased repeatedly and seeds do not expire. Use seeds for hints, revealing a fruit, extra hearts and level unlocks. Purchases require internet, are processed by Apple and verified through StoreKit. Delivery is saved on this device first, without activating a separate cloud wallet. With iCloud game sync enabled, the balance and records preventing duplicate delivery sync in the background with your save.

The optional, one-time Orchard Doku Pro purchase unlocks all 6,000 campaign levels and all 1,000 3D puzzles, includes a first-purchase gift of 120 seeds, and adds first-clear reward bonuses. Restore eligible Pro purchases from Settings.

MADE FOR IPHONE AND IPAD
Enjoy friendly fruit characters, optional music, sound and haptics, and iPad landscape layouts. Optional Game Center leaderboards compare total first-time campaign clears.

Available in English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Korean. Enjoy an optional dark mode and 3D fruit that stays upright as you tilt your device.

The first 5,000 levels retain adaptive difficulty. New levels 5,001–6,000 are 10×10 puzzles in a fixed order with gradually increasing difficulty.

5×5から10×10の盤面で、全6,000ステージに挑戦。最近のプレイに応じて難易度が変わります。独立したデイリーチャレンジもあり、通常ステージの盤面とメモは保存されます。

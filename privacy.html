1. Game data and optional iCloud sync

For version 1.1.5, iCloud sync is enabled by default on a new installation or when no saved choice exists; an existing off choice is respected. Change it in Settings > iCloud Sync. The private cloud save uses the Apple Account signed in to iCloud, independently of Game Center sign-in. Classic star records and 3D route progress and level access are included in the game save. Your game is saved on this device first. When sync is enabled and iCloud is available, campaign and daily progress, saved boards, golden seeds and transaction history, purchased hints, level access, adaptive difficulty, orchards and claimed-reward records are then saved in your Apple account’s private CloudKit database. Pro welcome-gift and backfill markers, including the original transaction identifier used to prevent duplicate rewards, travel with the complete save. Pro ownership is verified separately through the App Store and StoreKit. Language, music, sound, haptics and other device preferences, as well as practice progress, remain local. The app has no developer-hosted player account or advertising SDK. Usage analytics from version 1.2.2 is described below.

2. Purchases and the welcome gift

Apple processes in-app payments. StoreKit verifies Pro ownership and purchases of 120, 420 or 1,200 gold seeds. Consumable packs can be purchased repeatedly and seeds do not expire. The app stores transaction identifiers, delivery and reward records, the balance and transaction history to deliver purchases and avoid duplicate grants. Purchases require internet, are processed by Apple and verified through StoreKit. Delivery is saved on this device first, without activating a separate cloud wallet. With iCloud game sync enabled, the balance and records preventing duplicate delivery sync in the background with your save. Pro ownership remains subject to App Store verification. These records are not sent to a developer-operated server. The developer does not receive full payment card details.

2a. Game Center leaderboards

When signed in to Game Center, the app submits classic first-clear counts, perfect classic clear counts, the longest daily completion streak, the best Time Trial score and a unified count of distinct completed 3D levels. It also reports achievement identifiers and completion percentages, including 3D milestones and Desert Watch. Scores and achievement progress are associated with the signed-in Game Center player. Apple manages player identity, public profiles and rankings under your Game Center settings. The app uses no developer-operated leaderboard server and does not use these records for advertising or tracking. You can play without Game Center; linking an existing guest Time Trial best score requires your choice. Game Center & Privacy

2b. Time Trial records

The app saves your current Time Trial board and timing state, best scores associated with a local guest identifier or Game Center player identifier, and the date and count of daily free starts. If you enable iCloud game sync, best-score records and daily-start records are included in the save in your Apple account’s private CloudKit database. The active run and recent-puzzle queue stay on the current device and are not uploaded in that game-save snapshot. Sound, haptic and language preferences remain local.

2c. 3D progress and leaderboards

The current 3D route, completed levels, unfinished boards, unlocked access and reward records are stored locally and included in enabled iCloud game sync. Earlier size-specific Game Center leaderboards remain available for older app versions; version 1.1.5 reports to the unified 3D leaderboard. Desert Watch progress and earned skins remain local and are not included in iCloud game sync.

The request contains the app’s public store identifier, the selected app language, and a country code based on your App Store storefront or device region; the app does not attach game progress, purchase records or a custom player identifier. Apple receives the network request and handles it under its own privacy terms. Rating-prompt timing, usage counts, previously shown version notices and related preferences are kept locally. Ratings are requested through Apple’s system interface; the app does not collect the rating or review you submit.

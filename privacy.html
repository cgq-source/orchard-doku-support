Privacy policy

About this policy

Effective: September 22, 2026. This policy describes information handled by Guiquan Chen (cgq-source), the developer of Orchard Doku, in the app, support correspondence and this website. Contact: abc466254511@gmail.com.

1. Game data and optional iCloud sync

Your game is saved on this device first. You can opt in through Settings → iCloud Sync. Campaign and daily progress, saved boards, golden seeds and transaction history, purchased hints, level access, adaptive difficulty, orchards and claimed-reward records are then saved in your Apple account’s private CloudKit database. Pro welcome-gift and backfill markers, including the original transaction identifier used to prevent duplicate rewards, travel with the complete save. Pro ownership is verified separately through the App Store and StoreKit. Language, music, sound, haptics and other device preferences, as well as practice progress, remain local. The app has no developer-hosted player account, advertising SDK or third-party behavioral analytics service.

2. Purchases and the welcome gift

Apple processes in-app payments. StoreKit verifies Pro ownership and purchases of 120 or 420 gold seeds. Consumable packs can be purchased repeatedly and seeds do not expire. The app stores transaction identifiers, delivery and reward records, the balance and transaction history to deliver purchases and avoid duplicate grants. Once activated, the cloud wallet stores these records separately in your Apple account’s private CloudKit database. Purchases and delivery require internet and iCloud sign-in. Gameplay spending and earned rewards save locally and take effect immediately, including offline; wallet synchronization runs outside gameplay. Pro ownership remains subject to App Store verification. These records are not sent to a developer-operated server. The developer does not receive full payment card details.

2a. Game Center leaderboards

If you choose to use leaderboards, Orchard Doku uses Apple Game Center to submit your number of first-time campaign clears and display global and friend rankings. Apple manages your Game Center account and profile under your Game Center settings and privacy terms. Orchard Doku does not operate its own leaderboard server. You can continue playing locally without signing in to Game Center. Game Center associates leaderboard scores with your Game Center player identity and displays your profile according to your Apple settings. These scores are not used by us for advertising or tracking. Game Center & Privacy.

3. Email support

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you provide. We use them to reply and investigate your request, and do not use them for advertising. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information, payment details or passwords. Email providers process correspondence to deliver and store it. Support records are retained only as needed to resolve requests or meet applicable obligations.

4. Storage, deletion and restoration

Before changing devices, connect the old device and check that sync completed, then enable sync on the new device using the same iCloud account. If both saves differ on first sync or both devices have new changes, you choose and confirm one complete save. Both saves are backed up locally before replacement; these backups are not uploaded to iCloud. Disabling sync does not delete either save. Deleting the app may remove local data and backups; cloud data is managed through your iCloud account. You can play offline; real-time background sync is not guaranteed. Restore Purchases verifies Pro ownership, does not reissue the welcome gift, and does not replace game-save restoration. An activated cloud wallet synchronizes separately from game-save conflict selection. Before changing devices or deleting the app, verify that both game and wallet synchronization completed. Check pending purchases retries unfinished consumable delivery; it does not reissue previously consumed seeds.

5. This GitHub Pages website

This site uses GitHub Pages hosting. We do not add advertising scripts, analytics scripts, tracking cookies or forms. GitHub may process technical requests, including IP address and browser information, to serve and protect the site. Visiting a support or policy link does not upload game progress. GitHub describes its processing here: GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Sharing, international processing and your choices

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Apple, GitHub and email providers handle data for the services described above, which may involve processing outside your country under their policies. Where applicable, support correspondence is processed to respond to your request and for our legitimate interest in providing support, or to comply with legal obligations. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, obtain a portable copy, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and complain to a privacy regulator. Contact us to exercise rights concerning information we hold; Apple and GitHub handle requests concerning their own services.

7. Children and policy updates

The app does not ask players for their name, age, contact details or location. A parent or guardian may contact us about personal information a child has included in support correspondence. We will update this page and its effective date if our information practices change. Privacy questions: abc466254511@gmail.com.

1. ゲームデータと任意のiCloud同期

ゲームはまずこの端末に保存されます。「設定 → iCloud同期」で同期を有効にすると、メインとデイリーの進行状況、途中の盤面、金の種と収支履歴、購入済みヒント、ステージの解放状況、自動難易度、果樹園の実績と受取済み報酬を、AppleアカウントのプライベートCloudKitデータベースに保存します。Pro初回特典と追加報酬の記録（重複付与を防ぐ元の取引識別子を含む）もセーブデータと一緒に同期します。Proの所有権はApp StoreとStoreKitで別途確認します。言語、音楽、効果音、触覚などの端末設定と練習の進行状況は端末内に残ります。開発者独自のプレイヤーアカウント、広告SDK、第三者の行動分析サービスは使用しません。

2. 購入と初回特典

支払いはAppleが処理し、StoreKitでProの所有権と120個・420個のゴールドシード購入を検証します。パックは繰り返し購入でき、シードに有効期限はありません。購入の付与と重複付与の防止のため、取引識別子、付与と報酬の記録、残高、収支履歴を保存します。クラウドウォレットを有効にすると、これらはAppleアカウントのプライベートCloudKitデータベースに別の記録として保存されます。購入と付与にはインターネットとiCloudへのサインインが必要です。ゲーム内の使用と報酬はオフラインでも直ちに端末に保存・反映され、同期はゲーム外で行われます。Proの所有権はApp Storeで検証します。開発者の独自サーバーへ送信せず、開発者は支払いカード情報の全体を受け取りません。

2a. Game Centerランキング

ランキングを利用する場合、Orchard DokuはApple Game Centerを通じてメインステージの初回クリア数を送信し、世界と友達の順位を表示します。Game Centerのアカウントとプロフィールは、Game Centerの設定とプライバシー規約に従ってAppleが管理します。Orchard Dokuは独自のランキングサーバーを運営していません。Game Centerにサインインせずにローカルでプレイを続けることもできます。 Game CenterはスコアをプレイヤーのIDに関連付け、Appleの設定に従ってプロフィールを表示します。当方はこれらのスコアを広告やトラッキングに使用しません。 Game Center & Privacy.

4. 保存、削除、復元

端末を変える前に元の端末をオンラインにし、同期の完了を確認してください。新しい端末では同じiCloudアカウントで同期を有効にします。初回に異なるデータがある場合や、両方の端末で進んだ場合は、使うセーブデータを1つ選んで確認します。置き換える前に両方のデータを端末にバックアップし、このバックアップはiCloudに送信しません。同期をオフにしても両方のデータは残ります。アプリの削除により端末のデータやバックアップが失われる場合があります。クラウドのデータはiCloudアカウントで管理されます。オフラインでも遊べますが、バックグラウンドでの即時同期は保証しません。「購入を復元」はProの所有権を確認する機能で、初回特典の再付与やゲームデータの復元は行いません。 有効なクラウドウォレットはゲームデータの競合選択とは別に同期されます。端末変更やアプリ削除の前に、ゲームとウォレット両方の同期完了を確認してください。未付与購入の確認は未完了の消耗品付与を再試行し、消費済みシードの再付与は行いません。

1. Spieldaten und optionale iCloud-Synchronisierung

Dein Spiel wird zunächst auf diesem Gerät gespeichert. Unter Einstellungen → iCloud-Synchronisierung kannst du die Synchronisierung aktivieren. Kampagnen- und Tagesfortschritt, gespeicherte Spielfelder, goldene Samen und Verlauf, gekaufte Hinweise, Level-Freischaltungen, adaptive Schwierigkeit, Obstgärten und erhaltene Belohnungen werden dann in der privaten CloudKit-Datenbank deines Apple-Accounts gespeichert. Apple verarbeitet In-App-Zahlungen. StoreKit prüft Pro-Besitz und Käufe von 120 oder 420 Goldsamen. Pakete sind wiederholt kaufbar und die Samen verfallen nicht. Nach Aktivierung der Cloud-Geldbörse liegen diese Einträge separat in der privaten CloudKit-Datenbank deines Apple-Accounts. Kauf und Gutschrift benötigen Internet und eine iCloud-Anmeldung. Ausgaben und erspielte Belohnungen werden auch offline sofort lokal gespeichert und wirksam. Die Geldbörse synchronisiert außerhalb des Spiels. Pro-Besitz wird weiterhin vom App Store geprüft. Die Einträge werden nicht an einen eigenen Entwicklerserver gesendet. Der Entwickler erhält keine vollständigen Zahlungskartendaten.
